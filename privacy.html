Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy is issued in compliance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations. It describes how Abilisto collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal data in connection with your use of the Platform.

1. Data Collection and Lawful Purpose

Abilisto collects only data that is necessary, relevant, and proportionate to platform operations.

Clients

  • Name and contact details
  • Approximate or masked location data (for service matching only)

Workers

  • Government-issued identification
  • Professional certifications (e.g., TESDA National Certificates)
  • Verification and safety-related information

Data is processed solely for identity verification, fraud prevention, platform safety, and transaction facilitation.

2. Data Protection and Security Measures

Abilisto implements organizational, technical, and physical safeguards, including:

  • Encryption of sensitive documents
  • Access controls and role-based permissions
  • Secure storage consistent with industry best practices
Our Commitment Abilisto does not sell, rent, or commercially exploit personal data.

3. Financial Information

Abilisto does not store credit or debit card details, e-wallet credentials, or passwords. All payments are processed by Xendit, which complies with applicable financial security and regulatory standards.

4. Data Retention

Personal data is retained only:

  • For as long as necessary to fulfill platform purposes
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations
  • To resolve disputes or prevent fraud

5. Data Subject Rights

In accordance with Philippine law, users have the right to:

  • Access their personal data
  • Request correction or rectification
  • Request erasure or account deletion, subject to legal retention requirements

Requests may be denied when necessary to comply with legal obligations or ongoing disputes.

6. Lawful Disclosure

Personal data may be disclosed only when:

  • Required by law or court order
  • Necessary to protect public safety or prevent fraud
  • Mandated by authorized government agencies
Final Legal Notice Abilisto functions strictly as a neutral technology platform. Responsibility for service delivery, safety, and outcomes rests entirely with the contracting parties. The Platform's role is limited to verification, matchmaking, and infrastructure support.
